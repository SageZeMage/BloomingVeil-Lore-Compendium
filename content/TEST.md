##### Session One
Not yet a party, Dirk, Lucius, Lily, Heather, and Nym, all arrive at Celestine Academy individually an in their own way. Lily, a hamster, arrives via shipping crate. Heather Windcross, a spring Elidrin, arrived on a communal airship. Dirk Proudwing the Third, a human, has his three attendants following him; Hewey, Dewey, and Luie. Lucius Bonnet, a human, arrives via private airship, and while he seems to have been born into duty, he walks in defiance of it. Lastly, Nym Uladan, a sea elf, arrives via the fountain with help from her Uncle Bob, a kraken famous for his destructive nature. He sends her off and asks her to inform the headmaster he has to reschedule beer-pong ==(Why?)==. 
After a while, everyone enters into the building. However, instead of seeing the entrance hall of the building, they each are alone in a large dome and hear an announcement that it's a trail.
Dirk is in a room with a glowing red floor inscribed with glowing runes. On the other side of the room there are two stone buttons and plaque that reads "Press both, but press not the path beneath." between him and the buttons is some chains hanging from the ceiling and some floating blocks. Dirk tries to shoot the buttons with his crossbow but fails. He then steps on the floating blocks, which fall with his weight but do not trigger the floor, and uses them to parkour across the room and press the buttons.
